Villa security in Saint-Tropez and across the peninsula

Your property deserves more than an alarm. Our agents provide a human presence — deterrent and discreet — whether your villa is occupied in high season or closed all winter.

A presence tailored to each situation

A villa in the hills of Ramatuelle, a waterfront residence in Gassin, a property closed nine months a year: each estate has its own vulnerabilities. We always start with a site visit to size the detail — never the other way round.

What villa security includes

  • Static guarding with an agent on site — day, night or around the clock
  • Scheduled or random patrols, indoors and out, with a written report every time
  • Access control: household staff, contractors, deliveries, guests
  • Opening and closing the property to your instructions
  • Alarm verification and response, working with your monitoring company
  • Off-season surveillance of closed properties, systems checked on every visit

Why a local team changes everything

Based on the peninsula, we know the roads, the neighbourhoods and the rhythms of the Gulf. When an alert comes in, our response does not depend on a call centre 800 kilometres away. And our partnership with a locally established private chauffeur service gives us a coverage of the terrain no large group can replicate.

A strict legal framework

CNAPS-licensed company, agents holding the mandatory professional card, liability insurance up to date. Copies of our credentials come with every quote.

Patrols & alarm response, in detail

Patrols are the heart of villa security: scheduled or random visits, access and systems checks, a photo report every time. When an alarm triggers, verification happens within minutes by a local agent — the link that turns your monitoring contract into real protection.

The security audit — the recommended starting point

Before any detail, we offer an audit of your property: access points, lighting, vegetation, blind spots, staff habits. You receive concrete, prioritised recommendations — including those that earn us nothing, like a simple lighting adjustment. It is often the right place to start.

Villa security in Saint-Tropez: your questions

How much does villa security cost in Saint-Tropez?

Rates depend on patrol frequency, property size and season. We provide a personalized quote within 24 hours, after a site visit — never a guess over the phone.

Do you respond to alarms in the middle of the night?

Yes — our on-call covers 24/7, all year. Based on the peninsula, our response times are measured in minutes, not hours.

Do I need a year-round contract?

No. Full season, off-season only, or ad-hoc visits while you are away: the detail follows how you actually use the property.
