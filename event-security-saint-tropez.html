Event security in Saint-Tropez: parties, weddings, receptions

A successful event in Saint-Tropez is one where nobody notices the security. Our agents look after your guests with the restraint the peninsula demands.

From private parties to grand weddings

Private villa, beach, vineyard estate, yacht at berth: we secure events from 20 to several hundred guests, in high season and out. The detail is sized with you, based on the venue, the guest list and the level of exposure.

What the detail includes

  • Guest screening: invitation checks, list management, courteous turn-aways
  • Discreet guest management throughout the event
  • Protection of sensitive areas: cloakrooms, backstage, technical zones
  • Vehicle flow management and coordination with valets
  • Coordination with your vendors: caterer, DJ, planner, venue
  • A controlled end of night: staggered departures, escorted walk-outs

The chauffeur advantage: coordinated arrivals and departures

Our partnership with a private chauffeur service based on the peninsula makes the difference on busy nights. Guest arrivals, transfers from hotels, the heliport or the harbour, end-of-night departures: security and drivers work from the same brief, with a single point of contact for you.

Contractual confidentiality

Systematic confidentiality on the event itself, your guests' identities and everything our agents see or hear. It is a clause in our contracts, not a verbal promise.

The anatomy of a controlled evening

An event is secured the way it is staged: flows, posts, zones. This is the logic of a reception detail — every post receives a written brief before the first guest arrives.

Private reception — 3 posts, 1 controlled guest flow

Securing an event in Saint-Tropez: your questions

How many agents does my event need?

The industry baseline is around one agent per 50–75 guests, adjusted to the venue layout and guest profile. We always do a site reconnaissance before pricing — full guide here (in French).

Are your agents discreet in photos and videos?

Yes. Attire matched to your event's dress code, positioning away from photo lines, and contractual confidentiality on everything our agents see and hear.

Can security be arranged last minute?

Yes, subject to availability — our on-call makes it possible. In high season, the ideal is still to book 2–3 weeks ahead.
