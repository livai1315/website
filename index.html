Frequently asked

What clients ask us
before signing.

Are you a licensed security company?

Yes. We hold the operating licence issued by CNAPS (the French private-security regulator), our agents carry the mandatory professional card, and our liability insurance is current. Copies come with every quote — demand this from any provider.

Which area do you cover?

Saint-Tropez, Ramatuelle, Gassin, Grimaud, Port Grimaud, Cogolin, Sainte-Maxime and La Croix-Valmer for rapid response. Beyond on request — including escorting clients on their travels.

How fast can I get a quote?

Within 24 hours, weekends included in season. For any serious detail we prefer to visit the site before pricing — beware of quotes given over the phone without seeing the location.

One-off missions or year-round only?

Both. A single evening, a reception weekend, the full season or year-round surveillance of a property: the detail is sized to your actual needs, with no unnecessary commitment.

Do your agents speak English?

Yes — English-speaking agents are assigned to all international clients, and we can liaise directly with your family office or personal staff.

How does the security + chauffeur offer work?

Our partner runs a private chauffeur service based on the peninsula. Agents and drivers work from the same brief, with one point of contact and one quote: protection, transfers and event coordination without friction between providers.
