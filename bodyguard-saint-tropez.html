Covering the entire Gulf of Saint-Tropez

Based in Saint-Tropez (83990, France), we operate throughout the peninsula: Ramatuelle and Pampelonne beach, Gassin, Grimaud and Port-Grimaud, Cogolin, Sainte-Maxime, La Croix-Valmer and Cavalaire-sur-Mer. All our agents hold the French professional security licence (CNAPS) — the strict government accreditation required to operate legally in France.
